Lockouts we handle, any hour

  • Car lockouts. Keys locked inside, in the trunk, or lost. Every make and model, opened with the right tool for that door — no slim jims, no scratched paint.
  • House and apartment lockouts. Deadbolts, knobs, smart locks that died, doors that slammed behind you. Picked or bypassed, not drilled, in almost every case.
  • Business lockouts. Storefronts, offices, warehouses, storage units. We can also rekey on the spot if an employee or tenant still has a key.
  • Broken key in the lock. Extracted without damaging the cylinder, then a fresh key cut on the van.
  • Lock changes after a break-in. Emergency rekey or replacement so you can sleep that night, plus a free check of every other door.
  • Safe lockouts. Forgotten combination or dead keypad on a home or office safe.

What to do while you wait

  • Stay somewhere safe and well lit. If it's cold or late, wait in a nearby shop or a friend's car.
  • Have your ID ready. We verify that you own or have the right to enter the car, home or business before opening it.
  • Don't try to force the lock, the window or the door frame. The repair almost always costs more than the lockout.
