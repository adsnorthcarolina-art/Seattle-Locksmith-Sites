What rekeying is

Inside every pin-tumbler lock is a row of small pins that match the cuts on your key. Rekeying swaps those pins for a new set, so the old key no longer turns and a new key does. The lock itself — the deadbolt, the knob, the handle — stays exactly where it is. It costs a fraction of replacing the hardware and takes a few minutes per lock.

Rekey or replace?

Rekey if the lock is a decent brand in good condition. Replace if it's worn, damaged, a low-grade builder lock, or you want a smart lock or high-security cylinder. The technician will tell you which, lock by lock, before starting — and the estimate is free either way.
