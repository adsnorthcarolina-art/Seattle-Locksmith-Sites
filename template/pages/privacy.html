Information we collect

Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your information. It is shared only with service providers who help us run the business and are bound to use it only for that purpose: the form-delivery service that emails your request to our dispatcher (Web3Forms), our website host (Cloudflare), Google (Analytics, Tag Manager, Business Profile and Ads), our phone/SMS carrier, and payment processors when you pay by card or app. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Text messages (SMS)

If you text us or ask us to text you, we reply about your request only. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to end messages, or HELP for help. Your phone number and consent to receive texts are never shared with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics cookies help us count visits and understand which pages are useful. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or enable "Do Not Track" — the site works the same without cookies.

How long we keep it

Call-back requests are kept for up to 12 months so we can follow up on a quote. Invoices and job records are kept for as long as tax and licensing law requires (normally seven years). Analytics data is kept for the period set in Google Analytics (currently 14 months).
