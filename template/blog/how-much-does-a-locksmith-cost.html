How to avoid the bait-and-switch

  1. Ask for the total price range on the phone, not just the "service call" fee.
  2. Ask the company's name. If the person answering can't say it, hang up. Lead-generation call centers resell your call to whoever is closest.
  3. Get the firm price before the technician touches the lock. If it jumped from the phone quote, send them away.
  4. Check the Google reviews for the exact business name, not a lookalike.
