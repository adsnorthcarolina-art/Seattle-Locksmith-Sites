Panic Bar & Exit Device Installation – common questions

Does my business need a panic bar?

Fire code requires panic hardware on many commercial exit doors, especially for assembly, retail and educational occupancies. We install to code and can advise on your doors.

Can a panic bar be locked from outside?

Yes, with outside trim that lets staff enter with a key while the inside always opens with a push.

Do you repair existing exit devices?

Yes. Von Duprin, Detex, Adams Rite and others are repaired or replaced, including alarmed exit devices.

How fast can you get to me in Seattle?

Most calls in Seattle are reached within 20 to 40 minutes. Bob's Locksmith runs mobile units around the city 24 hours a day, and the dispatcher gives you an arrival time when you call 206-984-6356.

Do I get a price before you start?

Yes, always. The technician checks the lock or the car on arrival and quotes the whole job before touching anything. The estimate is free and there are no hidden fees.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Bob's Locksmith is licensed and insured, in business since 2009, and every technician is background-checked and shows ID on arrival.
