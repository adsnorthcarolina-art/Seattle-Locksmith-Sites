Smart Lock Installation – common questions

Which smart locks do you install?

August, Yale, Schlage Encode, Kwikset Halo, Level, Ultraloq and most others. We can also recommend one that fits your door and your phone.

Will a smart lock work on my existing door?

Almost always. We check the door thickness, backset and alignment, and most smart locks replace a standard deadbolt without new holes.

Do you set it up with my phone and Wi-Fi?

Yes. We install the lock, pair it with your phone, add user codes and test it before we leave.

How fast can you get to me in Seattle?

Most calls in Seattle are reached within 20 to 40 minutes. Fast Locksmith runs mobile units around the city 24 hours a day, and the dispatcher gives you an arrival time when you call 206-317-4890.

Do I get a price before you start?

Yes, always. The technician checks the lock or the car on arrival and quotes the whole job before touching anything. The estimate is free and there are no hidden fees.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Fast Locksmith is licensed and insured, in business since 2011, and every technician is background-checked and shows ID on arrival.
