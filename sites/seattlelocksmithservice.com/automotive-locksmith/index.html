Car locksmith services

  • Car lockout. Keys locked in the car or trunk. Opened with manufacturer-specific tools so the door, window and weather seal are untouched.
  • Lost car keys — all keys lost. We cut a new key from the VIN or the lock and program it to the car, even when there is no key to copy. More on car key replacement.
  • Key fob and transponder programming. Remote-head keys, smart keys and push-to-start fobs programmed on site. Spare keys made while you wait.
  • Ignition repair and replacement. Key won't turn, turns but won't start, or is stuck in the ignition. Cylinders repaired or replaced and keyed to your existing key.
  • Broken key extraction. Snapped in the door or ignition. Removed without damaging the lock, then a new key cut.
  • Door lock and trunk lock repair. Worn cylinders, frozen locks, lock actuators.
  • Motorcycle, fleet and commercial vehicle keys. Including work vans and box trucks.

Makes and models

Toyota, Honda, Ford, Chevrolet, Nissan, Hyundai, Kia, Subaru, Mazda, Jeep, Dodge, RAM, GMC, Lexus, Acura, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Volkswagen and Tesla, among others. If it's on the road in Seattle, we almost certainly have the key blank and the programming tool for it.

Why not the dealer?

A dealer needs the car towed in, usually books days out, and charges for the tow, the key and the programming separately. Seattle Locksmith Service comes to the car in Seattle, makes the key on the spot, and quotes the whole job before starting. The free estimate includes the key, the cutting and the programming.
